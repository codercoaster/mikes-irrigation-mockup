Good questions

Frequently asked.

Can't find what you're looking for?
Call 281‑948‑4343 — Michael is happy to help.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Our Texas Irrigator License is #8192 and we carry liability insurance. In Texas it's against the law for anyone to install a sprinkler system on your property unless they are licensed.

How long does a sprinkler installation take?

Between 1 and 2 days, depending on the complexity of the design.

How deep are the trenches?

Normal depth for most trenches is 8–12 inches. We use equipment that keeps disruption to your lawn to a minimum.

What if a utility line gets damaged?

If we damage a utility line during installation, Michael's Irrigation & Landscaping is responsible for the repair.

Do I need to be home during the installation?

No. We only need access to where the controller will be mounted, usually the garage or inside the house.

What warranty do you offer?

A full 3‑year warranty against defects in materials and 1 year on workmanship.
